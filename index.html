What comes out of the oven, and when

5:00

Scroll through our morning. Come at the right time and it's still warm.

  1. 5:00

    The ovens go on

    Dough mixed yesterday has proved overnight. Ruth scores the first loaves while the street is still dark.

  2. 6:30

    Country sourdough

    Our main loaf: wheat, a little rye, 36 hours from flour to crust. Around 60 a day.

  3. 7:00

    Doors open

    Coffee on, first loaves on the shelf. Pre-orders are ready to collect from now.

  4. 8:30

    Cardamom buns

    Rolled, knotted and glazed with cardamom syrup. These usually sell out first.

  5. 11:00

    Focaccia and sandwiches

    Rosemary focaccia comes out for the lunch sandwiches: ham and pickles, or roast squash.

  6. 16:00

    Last loaf, then home

    Anything left goes half price after 15:00 or to the Mill Lane food bank.
